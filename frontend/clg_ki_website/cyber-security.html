CSE - Cyber Security

The Department of CSE – Cyber Security was established in the year 2022 with an initial intake of 30 students, which has now been increased to 60 students. The Department of Information Technology was established in the year 2024 with an initial intake of 60 students. The department provides a unique learning environment through innovative teaching methodologies.

The department is well-equipped with modern infrastructural facilities and supported by highly qualified and experienced faculty members. It focuses on imparting strong technical knowledge and practical skills in the latest technologies and tools through structured training and expert guidance.

To produce industry-ready professionals, the department regularly organizes industrial visits, expert lectures, workshops, and hands-on semester break training programs. It also emphasizes the development of moral and ethical values through various academic and extracurricular activities. Teamwork and leadership qualities are nurtured by actively involving students in technical and co-curricular activities.

The department consistently stays at the forefront of emerging technologies and continuously updates itself with the latest advancements in the field.

Vision

To be a globally recognized leader in the field of Computer Science and Cyber Security, fostering the development of innovative, ethical, and highly skilled professionals who will contribute to a secure digital future by addressing the challenges of cyber threats and building a cyber-resilient world.

Mission

  • Mission 1: To provide an innovative and research-driven educational platform with state-of-the-art infrastructure and expert faculty that equips students with the theoretical and practical knowledge of Computer Science and Cyber Security.
  • Mission 2: To foster a collaborative, project-based learning environment that empowers students to tackle global Computer Science and Cyber Security challenges, contributing to the safety of digital systems and networks.
  • Mission 3: To produce ethically grounded, highly skilled graduates who will protect the digital world by understanding, analyzing, and mitigating Computer Science concepts and cyber threats through analytical thinking, technical proficiency, and continuous professional development.

Programme Educational Objectives

PEO-01 Develop a strong foundation in Science, Mathematics, and Computing to apply Computer Science and Cyber Security concepts effectively in solving real-world problems.
PEO-02 Equip students with practical knowledge of Computer Science and Cyber Security tools, techniques, and strategies, enabling them to proactively identify, defend, and recover from cyber threats.
PEO-03 Prepare graduates to contribute as responsible professionals in interdisciplinary teams, developing innovative solutions to emerging Computer Science and Cyber Security challenges while adhering to ethical standards.

Programme Specific Outcomes

PSO-01 Demonstrate a comprehensive understanding of Computer Science and Cyber Security principles, tools, and techniques, and apply them to safeguard software, hardware, and digital systems from evolving cyber threats.
PSO-02 Contribute to cutting-edge research and develop solutions that address the societal and technological impacts of Computer Science and Cyber Security, enhancing digital trust and security across industries.
PSO-03 Exhibit ethical and professional responsibility by adhering to industry best practices, using standard tools, and applying sustainable and innovative solutions to solve complex Computer Science and Cyber Security issues.

Program Outcomes

PO-01 Engineering Knowledge: Apply the knowledge of mathematics, science, computing fundamentals, and Cyber Security specialization to solve complex engineering and cyber security problems.
PO-02 Problem Analysis: Identify, formulate, review research literature, and analyze complex Computer Science and Cyber Security problems using principles of mathematics, natural sciences, and engineering sciences to reach substantiated conclusions.
PO-03 Design/Development of Solutions: Design secure systems, components, or processes to solve complex engineering and cyber security problems with appropriate consideration for public health, safety, cultural, societal, and environmental factors.
PO-04 Conduct Investigations of Complex Problems: Use research-based knowledge and methods including design of experiments, data analysis, interpretation, and synthesis to provide valid conclusions for complex computing and cyber security issues.
PO-05 Modern Tool Usage: Create, select, and apply appropriate techniques, resources, and modern engineering and IT tools including modeling, simulation, and cyber security tools with an understanding of their limitations.
PO-06 The Engineer and Society: Apply contextual knowledge to assess societal, health, safety, legal, and cultural issues and understand the responsibilities relevant to professional Computer Science and Cyber Security practices.
PO-07 Environment and Sustainability: Understand the impact of engineering and cyber security solutions in societal and environmental contexts and demonstrate knowledge of sustainable development.
PO-08 Ethics: Apply ethical principles and commit to professional ethics, responsibilities, and norms of engineering and cyber security practice.
PO-09 Individual and Team Work: Function effectively as an individual, and as a member or leader in diverse and multidisciplinary teams.
PO-10 Communication: Communicate effectively on complex engineering and cyber security activities with the professional community and society through reports, documentation, presentations, and clear instructions.
PO-11 Project Management and Finance: Demonstrate knowledge of engineering and management principles and apply them as a member or leader to manage projects in multidisciplinary environments.
PO-12 Life-Long Learning: Recognize the need for independent and lifelong learning to adapt to emerging technologies and evolving cyber security challenges.

Scope and Opportunities

Data Security Council of India (DSCI), which is one of the top associations for cyber security has predicted that the cyber security market will employ around 10 lacks employees by 2025.

According to the senior director of NASSCOM, while the government’s initiatives such as Digital India and demonetization have pushed companies towards digital transformation, doing so has also made them vulnerable to cyber-attacks. In turn, this has led to a demand for cyber security professionals who can help companies achieve digital transformation without compromising security.

According to a report from PricewaterhouseCoopers, India’s cyber security market is predicted to grow from USD 1.97 billion in 2019 to USD 3.05 billion by 2022, with a rate one and a half times than the global rate.

Despite having the most extensive IT talent pool in the world, today, India severely lacks skilled cyber security professionals. This acute shortage has resulted in companies’ willingness to pay a premium salary to cyber security professionals. According to some reports, the average cyber security experts’ salaries in India have grown to INR. 8.8 lacks.

According to a report by the National Association of Software and Services Companies, there is a shortage of 1 million cyber security personnel to deal with cyber threats. As a result, there has never been a better time for any young person wishing to build a name for themselves in this field.
