Computer Science & Information Technology

The Department of Computer Science & Information Technology is committed to delivering quality education and fostering innovation among students. Established in the year 2024 with an initial intake of 60 students, the department provides a dynamic and enriching learning environment through various innovative teaching methodologies.

The department is well-equipped with modern infrastructural facilities and supported by highly qualified and experienced faculty members. It focuses on equipping students with strong fundamentals and practical knowledge of the latest technologies and tools through effective training and continuous guidance.

To produce industry-ready professionals, the department regularly organizes industrial visits, expert lectures, and hands-on training programs during semester breaks. Along with technical excellence, the department emphasizes the development of moral and ethical values through various activities and interactive sessions.

A strong spirit of teamwork and collaboration is encouraged by engaging students in technical, co-curricular, and extracurricular activities. The department consistently strives to stay updated with emerging trends and advancements in technology, ensuring students are well-prepared for future challenges.

Vision

To become a centre of excellence in Information Technology by producing globally competent and skilled professionals with strong ethical, social, and professional values, capable of developing innovative and secure digital solutions for societal and industrial needs.

Mission

  • Mission 1: To provide a strong academic foundation through quality teaching–learning processes and an industry-oriented curriculum.
  • Mission 2: To promote research, innovation, and problem-solving abilities by encouraging hands-on learning, projects, and interdisciplinary practices.
  • Mission 3: To develop globally competent professionals by enhancing technical expertise, communication skills, and proficiency in emerging information technologies.
  • Mission 4: To nurture responsible professionals with strong ethical values, social commitment, and awareness of environmental sustainability in digital practices.

Programme Educational Objectives

PEO-01 Graduates will build successful careers in information technology by applying strong technical knowledge, analytical abilities, modern tools, and problem-solving skills developed through a quality, industry-oriented academic foundation.
PEO-02 Graduates will demonstrate effective communication, leadership qualities, ethical values, sustainability awareness, and a sense of responsibility toward society and the environment while working in diverse professional environments.
PEO-03 Graduates will excel in higher education, research, corporate careers, or entrepreneurial ventures by engaging in lifelong learning and continuous professional development.

Programme Specific Outcomes

PSO-01 Students will be able to design, develop, and implement efficient and secure IT solutions by applying strong academic foundations, modern development tools, and contemporary technologies.
PSO-02 Students will be able to apply analytical thinking, engineering principles, and practical skills to solve real-world problems, undertake innovative interdisciplinary projects, and contribute to research-driven advancements in information technology.
PSO-03 Students will be able to demonstrate professional ethics, teamwork, effective communication, creativity, and a strong sense of social and environmental responsibility while delivering sustainable and socially relevant IT solutions.

Program Outcomes

PO-01 Engineering Knowledge: Apply knowledge of mathematics, natural science, computing, engineeringfundamentals and an engineering specialization as specified in WK1 to WK4 respectively to develop the solution of complex engineering problems.
PO-02 Problem Analysis: Identify, formulate, review research literature and analyze complex engineering problems reaching substantiated conclusions with consideration for sustainable development. (WK1 to WK4)
PO-03 Design/Development of Solutions: Design creative solutions for complex engineering problems and design/develop systems, components, or processes to meet identified needs with consideration for the public health and safety, whole-life cost, net zero carbon, culture, society and environment as required. (WK5)
PO-04 Conduct Investigations of Complex Problems: Conduct investigations of complex engineering problems using research-based knowledge including the design of experiments, modelling, analysis and interpretation of data to provide valid conclusions. (WK8)
PO-05 Engineering Tool Usage: Create, select and apply appropriate techniques, resources and modern engineering & IT tools, including prediction and modelling recognizing their limitations to solve complex engineering problems. (WK2 and WK6)
PO-06 The Engineer and The World: Analyze and evaluate societal and environmental aspects while solving complex engineering problems for its impact on sustainability with reference to economy, health, safety, legal framework, culture and environment. (WK1, WK5, and WK7)
PO-07 Ethics: Apply ethical principles and commit to professional ethics, human values, diversity and inclusion; adhere to national & international laws. (WK9)
PO-08 Function effectively as an individual, and as a member or leader in diverse/multi-disciplinary teams.
PO-09 Communication: Communicate effectively and inclusively within the engineering community and society at large, such as being able to comprehend and write effective reports and design documentation, make effective presentations considering cultural, language, and learning differences.
PO-10 Project Management and Finance: Apply knowledge and understanding of engineering management principles and economic decision-making and apply these to one’s own work, as a member and leader in a team, and to manage projects and in multidisciplinary environments.
PO-11 Recognize the need for, and have the preparation and ability for independent and life-long learning, adaptability to new and emerging technologies, and critical thinking in the broadest context of technological change. (WK8)

Scope and Opportunities

The field of Computer Science & Information Technology (CS & IT) is one of the fastest-growing and most dynamic domains in today’s digital era. With continuous advancements in computing technologies, software systems, and digital infrastructure, the demand for skilled CS & IT professionals is increasing rapidly across industries.

According to NASSCOM, India’s digital transformation is accelerating due to initiatives like Digital India, e-governance, and the widespread adoption of online services. This rapid shift has significantly increased the demand for professionals who can design, develop, and manage modern computing systems efficiently.

Reports by PricewaterhouseCoopers highlight that India’s technology sector is expanding steadily, driven by the growing adoption of cloud computing, data analytics, and automation. As organizations continue to invest in digital solutions, new opportunities are consistently being created for CS & IT graduates.

In terms of market growth, India’s IT industry is expected to reach approximately USD 350 billion by 2026, reflecting strong expansion and global competitiveness. This growth is accompanied by a rising demand for skilled professionals, with the IT workforce projected to reach nearly 6 million professionals in the near future.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is playing a vital role in CS & IT by enabling automation, intelligent decision-making, and advanced data processing, thereby enhancing system efficiency and driving innovation across digital platforms.

Despite having a large talent pool, there remains a noticeable gap in industry-ready professionals with strong technical and problem-solving skills. This gap has resulted in enhanced employability prospects and competitive salary packages for graduates in this field.

As per insights from the Data Security Council of India, the digital ecosystem in India is expected to generate employment opportunities in the millions, further strengthening the demand for CS & IT expertise.

Global projections from the Bureau of Labor Statistics also indicate a strong upward trend in technology-related roles, ensuring long-term career stability and growth potential in Computer Science & Information Technology.

The continuous evolution of computing technologies ensures that CS & IT remains a future-oriented field with vast scope, offering opportunities for innovation, research, and professional growth in both national and international markets.

Eligibility

JEE Exam & Online Counseling- The candidate from MP must have passed 10+2 Exam from Madhya Pradesh Board or any other Board with Physics, Chemistry as the main subjects and Mathematics/ Biology/ Biotechnology as one of the optional subjects, with minimum 45% marks for General category candidates and 40% in case of SC/ST candidate.

The candidate from other states must have passed 10+2 Exam from any recognized board with Physics, Chemistry as the main subjects and Mathematics/ Biology/ Biotechnology as one of the optional subjects, with 45% marks for General category candidates as well as SC/ST candidate. The candidate who has not given JEE exam but fulfils above criteria may also go for online counselling in the 2nd phase of counselling. Direct Admissions based on 12th results (College level Counseling)- The Candidate must have passed 10+2 Exam from Madhya Pradesh Board or any other Board with Physics, Chemistry as the main subjects and Mathematics/ Biology/ Biotechnology as one of the optional subjects, with minimum 50% marks for General category candidates and 45% in case of SC/ST candidate.

Frequently Asked Questions(FAQs)

1) What is the intake for B.Tech in Computer Science & Information Technology?

The intake for B.Tech in Computer Science & Information Technology is 60 students.

2) Are Computer Science & Information Technology in demand?

Yes, CS & IT are among the most in-demand fields in India and globally, driven by rapid growth in software development, artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and data science.

3) What is the salary of a Computer Science & Information Technology graduate in India?

The salary of a CS & IT graduate in India generally ranges from INR 3 LPA to INR 20+ LPA, depending on skills, experience, and company. The average starting salary is around INR 4–8 LPA.

4) Is coding required for Computer Science & Information Technology?

Yes, coding is a fundamental skill in both CS & IT. Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, Java, or C++ is essential for most roles.

5) Is networking required for Computer Science & Information Technology?

Yes, a basic understanding of networking concepts is important, especially for roles related to system administration, cybersecurity, and cloud computing.

6) What are the highest paying jobs in Computer Science & Information Technology in India?

Some of the highest paying roles include:

  • Software Developer / Engineer
  • Data Scientist
  • Cloud Engineer
  • AI/ML Engineer
  • DevOps Engineer
  • Cyber Security Specialist
  • Solution Architect

7) What are the job profile options after a Computer Science & Information Technology course?

Graduates can pursue various roles such as:

  • Software Engineer
  • Web Developer
  • Mobile App Developer
  • Data Analyst / Data Scientist
  • System Administrator
  • Network Engineer
  • Cloud Engineer
  • DevOps Engineer
  • AI/ML Engineer
  • Cyber Security Analyst
  • Database Administrator

8) What are the top companies that provide jobs for Computer Science & Information Technology graduates?

Some of the top recruiters include:

  • TCS
  • Infosys
  • Wipro
  • Cognizant
  • Accenture
  • Deloitte
  • Capgemini
  • HCL Technologies
