IoT and Measurement Science

Centre of Excellence in IoT and Measurement Science

The Embedded Systems and IoT Track under GAIA Smart Cities provides a hands-on, industry-oriented learning ecosystem for students, researchers, academicians, and professionals. The program covers PCB design, embedded firmware development, microcontroller interfacing, IoT connectivity, cloud integration, and robotics using industry-relevant platforms. Participants gain practical exposure through project-based learning to design and deploy intelligent connected solutions for smart-city applications.

Tools/Platform used:

Arduino UNO, Arduino IDE, Altium Designer, Renesas QuickConnect Kit V2.0, QCS, Renesas IDE, Renesas FSP, ESP32, MicroPython, Thonny IDE, ThingSpeak

Join us to explore, innovate, and build the next generation of semiconductor solutions. For access requests and further information, please contact the Centre of Excellence in IoT and Measurement Science.
