Management

With a legacy of more than 30 years, Sagar Group of Institutions (SISTec) believes in moving forward towards quality and excellence. SISTec School of Management & Business Studies is one of the Best MBA Colleges in Bhopal, M.P. The institute was formed with a vision to deliver top-notch quality education and prepare responsible future leaders to meet global corporate challenges.

Our team of well-known philanthropists, academicians, and dedicated staff brings in significant experience in teaching and training. We, at SISTec Business School, are committed to impart best-in-class knowledge with an appropriate blend of practical exposure creating future board room members.

Vision

To be an innovative and ever-evolving business school that delivers transformative education, cultivating leaders who are equipped to tackle business challenges, enhance societal well-being, and contribute to the nation’s growth.

Mission

  • To provide a dynamic learning environment that integrates rigorous academic knowledge with practical industry exposure, ensuring the development of competent management professionals.
  • To encourage critical thinking and innovative problem-solving by fostering a culture of research and knowledge creation relevant to global and local business contexts.
  • To nurture leadership, teamwork, and interpersonal skills through experiential learning, mentoring, and exposure to diverse cultural and professional environments.
  • To instill ethical values, social responsibility, and sustainability principles in students, equipping them to address complex challenges in the global business landscape.
  • To build strong industry-academic linkages, ensuring curriculum relevance and providing students with cutting-edge insights and career opportunities.

Programme Specific Outcomes

PSO-01 Graduates will possess the ability to analyze qualitative and quantitative data to inform and guide business decisions, utilizing tools and methodologies from various management disciplines to support evidence-based conclusions.
PSO-02 Graduates will proficiently communicate ideas and concepts clearly and persuasively, while demonstrating effective teamwork and interpersonal skills that facilitate collaboration in diverse professional settings.

Programme Educational Objectives

PEO-01 Graduates will demonstrate a solid foundation in management principles and practices, enabling them to effectively apply their knowledge to address complex business challenges and enhance organizational performance in diverse sectors.
PEO-02 Graduates will cultivate a strong sense of ethical leadership and social responsibility, empowering them to lead teams and organizations with integrity and make decisions that positively impact society and the environment.
PEO-03 Graduates will exhibit innovative and strategic thinking skills, allowing them to adapt to changing global business landscapes, foster creativity, and contribute to sustainable growth initiatives within their organizations.

Program Outcomes

PO-01 Apply knowledge of management theories and practices to solve business problems.
PO-02 Foster Analytical and critical thinking abilities for data-based decision making.
PO-03 Ability to develop Value based Leadership ability.
PO-04 Ability to understand, analyze and communicate global, economic, legal, and ethical aspects of business.
PO-05 Ability to lead themselves and others in the achievement of organizational goals, contributing effectively to a team environment.

BBA Programme

The BBA program - in accordance with NEP (New Education Policy) - affiliated with Barkatullah University emphasizes industry exposure while fostering a supportive and engaging learning environment. The assessment framework is comprehensive, incorporating examinations, presentations, case studies, and project work to ensure holistic evaluation.

With internships embedded in the curriculum to provide hands-on experience, and projects focused on real-world applications, the program equips students with the practical skills and knowledge required to confidently pursue entry-level roles in their chosen domains.
