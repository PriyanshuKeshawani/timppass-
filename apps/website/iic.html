About Us

The Ministry of Education (MoE), Government of India, has established the MoE's Innovation Cell (MIC) to foster a culture of innovation within Higher Education Institutions (HEIs) across the country.

The core mission of MIC is to inspire, motivate, and support young students by helping them explore and develop new ideas, ultimately transforming these ideas into viable prototypes during their formative academic years.

To achieve this goal, MIC has introduced the concept of Institution's Innovation Councils (IICs) within selected HEIs. These councils form a nationwide network aimed at promoting innovation and entrepreneurship through various approaches, contributing to the creation of a dynamic innovation ecosystem on campuses.

MoE's Innovation Cell (MIC) has successfully established IICs in over 2,700 institutions across India, with the goal of nurturing creativity and fostering an environment conducive to innovation-driven growth in educational institutions.

Primary Objective

The primary focus of the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell (MIC) includes:

  • Creating a vibrant local innovation ecosystem: Fostering an environment in HEIs that promotes creativity and collaboration for innovative ideas.
  • Supporting start-ups within HEIs: Establishing mechanisms to guide and mentor aspiring entrepreneurs, enabling them to develop successful start-ups.
  • Preparing institutes for ARIIA: Helping institutions align with the Atal Ranking of Institutions on Innovation Achievements framework.
  • Establishing an ecosystem for idea scouting and pre-incubation: Providing structured support to identify and nurture early-stage ideas into workable solutions.
  • Developing students' cognitive abilities: Enhancing problem-solving and creative thinking skills among students to thrive in an innovation-driven environment.

Operations of IIC

Functions of the Institution's Innovation Council (IIC):

  • Conducting innovation, IPR, and entrepreneurship activities: Implementing various programs related to innovation, intellectual property rights, and entrepreneurship as prescribed by MIC within specified timelines.
  • Identifying and rewarding innovations: Recognizing outstanding innovations and sharing success stories to inspire others.
  • Organizing workshops and mentoring sessions: Hosting workshops, seminars, and interactions with entrepreneurs, investors, and professionals, while building a mentor network for student innovators.
  • Networking with peers and organizations: Establishing connections with peer institutions and national entrepreneurship development bodies.
  • Creating an Innovation Portal: Developing a portal to showcase innovative projects by faculty and students.
  • Hosting competitions and challenges: Organizing hackathons, idea competitions, and mini-challenges with active industry participation.
