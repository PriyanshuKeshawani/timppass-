Electronics & Communication Engineering

The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering was established in 2007 with the objective of providing quality education and developing competent engineers capable of addressing modern technological challenges. The department offers a dynamic learning environment that integrates theoretical knowledge with practical applications, focussing on current industry requirements and emerging technologies.

The department is supported by experienced and dedicated faculty members who are committed to excellence in teaching, research, and innovation. Continuous efforts are made to enhance students’ technical competence, analytical ability, and professional skills.

The department is well equipped with modern infrastructure and well-established laboratories that enable students to gain hands-on experience and practical exposure in various areas of Electronics and Communication Engineering.

To promote innovation, research, and industry-oriented learning, the department has established the following Centers of Excellence (CoE):

  • Center of Excellence in IoT and Measurement Science – focusing on smart sensing technologies, embedded systems, IoT-based applications, and advanced instrumentation.
  • Center of Excellence in Semiconductor Technology – promoting semiconductor design, VLSI research, chip design training, and innovation aligned with the national Chips to Startup (C2S) initiative.

These centers provide students with opportunities to work on advanced projects, research activities, and industry-driven applications.

The department actively encourages students to participate in projects, internships, industrial visits, expert lectures, workshops, and technical competitions to enhance their practical knowledge and professional exposure.

To foster technical creativity and leadership skills, the department runs the “Electro-Pulse” Technical Club, which organizes technical activities, competitions, and innovation-driven events for students. The department also has an active IETE Student Chapter, which connects students with the professional body The Institution of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineers (IETE) and promotes technical learning through seminars, workshops, and professional activities.

Graduates from the department find opportunities in diverse sectors such as electronics manufacturing, telecommunications, automation, IoT, robotics, semiconductor industries, research & development, and IT sectors.

Vision

To become a centre of excellence in Electronics and Communication Engineering, producing competent professionals through quality education, relevant training, industry collaboration and innovation; also imparting ethical, social and national values for sustainable growth.

Mission

  • To impart strong fundamentals of Electronics and Communication Engineering with modern teaching methodologies, relevant infrastructure and practical exposure.
  • To foster creativity, innovation, and an entrepreneurial mindset through skill enhancement and to promote collaborative learning with industries and research organizations for addressing real-world challenges and sustainable technological development.
  • To inculcate lifelong learning, professional ethics, and leadership qualities for global employability.

Programme Educational Objectives

PEO-01 Graduates will have sound knowledge of Electronics and Communication fundamentals to analyze, design, and implement real-world solutions.
PEO-02 Graduates will be pursuing higher studies, job or entrepreneurship.
PEO-03 Graduates will demonstrate leadership skill, teamwork, effective communication skill, ethical values, and social responsibility to contribute for sustainable technological growth.

Programme Specific Outcomes

PSO-01 Apply the principles of Electronics and Communication Engineering to design, analyze, and implement efficient electronic systems and communication networks.
PSO-02 Use modern tools, programming techniques, and emerging technologies such as VLSI, embedded systems, IoT, and wireless communication to develop innovative engineering solutions.
PSO-03 To enable the students with social and environmental awareness along with professional ethics to have a successful career with an entrepreneurial mindset.

M.Tech. VLSI Design
Current Intake : 18

M.Tech in VLSI Design is a two-year postgraduate programme that aims to impart knowledge of VLSI system design, covering algorithms, hardware description languages, system architectures, physical designs, verification techniques, simulation and synthesis, low power design techniques etc.

The recent advancements in VLSI design have enabled most systems to become compact and reliable and to deliver data at high speed. With the advent of VLSI designs, the number of applications of integrated circuits (ICs) in high-performance computing, controls, telecommunications, image and video processing, and consumer electronics has been rising at a fast pace. Therefore, there is good scope and high demand for engineers in the fast-changing chip designing industry.

Automatic Hand Sanitizer

Demand for hand sanitizers has surged since the corona virus broke out and spread around the world. Hand sanitizers are usually applied by squirting the sanitizer liquid when one presses a pump with one’s hand. This causes many people to come into contact with the pump handle, which increases the risk of viral transmission. Some hand sanitizers on the market are automatically pumped. However, because sanitizer containers and pump devices are designed to be compatible only between products produced by the same manufacturer, consumers must also repurchase the container for the liquid if they replace the hand sanitizer. Therefore, this paper suggests the design of an automatic hand sanitizer system compatible with various sanitizer containers. COVID-19 is the greatest threat we are facing now. Using masks, gloves, sanitizing our hands, getting checked for Temperature, Pulse rate and Oxygen level has become our new normal. However, in the public places people are still at risk when they use conventional sanitizer dispensers and allotting a person with temperature gun, who himself is at the risk of getting affected by COVID. Our Sensor based Automated System will be a better solution for the current scenario and it is risk free.
